About GYMZONE

TRAIN WITH A PLAN

A practical exercise guide for people who are starting or rebuilding their gym routine and want clear technique before heavy weights.

OUR GOAL

Many beginners walk into a gym without knowing which exercises to choose or how to perform them safely. GYMZONE collects the basic movements in one place, shows each one as a short animation and explains the technique in three steps.

The site is a student project for the Web Technologies course at Astana IT University. It works without a backend: profiles and saved exercises stay in your browser.

WHO IT IS FOR

01

Beginners

People in their first months of training who need simple, safe exercises.

02

Students

University students who train between classes and want a quick plan.

03

Returning lifters

Anyone coming back after a break who wants to refresh their technique.
